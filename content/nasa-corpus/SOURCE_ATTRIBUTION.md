# NASA, JPL & CCSDS Public-Domain Source Attribution (`Quiet UI v8.0`)

All technical reference parameters, protocol structures, and orbital imagery used as
sample content across the 16 Quiet UI tiers and 45-Type Diagram Atlas derive from
publicly released United States Government works (`17 U.S.C. § 105`) and open CCSDS
Consultative Committee for Space Data Systems Blue Books.

> **Non-Endorsement Notice (`14 CFR Part 1221`):** Use of public-domain NASA technical
> memoranda, DSN handbooks, and Apollo imagery is strictly for open-source UI/UX and
> human-factors engineering demonstration and does not imply endorsement by NASA, JPL,
> Caltech, or CCSDS.

## 2. Standards, Handbooks & Optical Viewport Imagery

- **CCSDS 133.0-B-2 & MRN Handbook v4** (`doc-3,doc-4a,app-tree`): Space Packet Protocol (6-byte primary header, APID 11-bit, 14-bit sequence count) and Proximity-1 UHF/Optical relay framing. ([Source](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/psd/mars/files/mars-relay-network/MRN%20Handbook_v4_final_URS_cleared.pdf))
- **DSN 810-005 Telecommunications Link Design Handbook** (`bridge-2,app-2,app-3`): Goldstone (GDSCC), Madrid (MDSCC), and Canberra (CDSCC) 34m BWG and 70m aperture telemetry & command parameters. ([Source](https://deepspace.jpl.nasa.gov/dsndocs/810-005/))
- **NASA-STD-3001 Vol 2 Rev C & HIDH (NASA/SP-2010-3407)** (`app-5,hud-1,ctrl-1`): Human-factors display/effector distinction, commanded-vs-actual state, 2-step guarded actuation, cabin 70-101.3 kPa & 25-75% RH. ([Source](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/2022-04-08_nasa-std-3001_vol_2_rev_c_final.pdf))
