Quiet UI Pillar 3 governs audience-targeted translation, scrub of presenter bias, and adversarial red-teaming across seven audience lenses and three operational decision tempos.

---

## 1. The Asymmetric Communication Vector & Numerical Conservation

Technical communication is not symmetric. Presenters habitually overload artifacts with implementation details while omitting the proof the receiver requires:

$$\vec{V} = \text{Presenter Source} \longrightarrow \text{Audience Lens} \times \text{Decision Tempo}$$

### The Numerical Conservation Law
When projecting a system across multiple audience views, the underlying physical truth (parameters, limits, citations, and timestamps) MUST derive from a single canonical JSON dataset. Presentation changes framing, pacing, and coordinate density—never the underlying numbers.
