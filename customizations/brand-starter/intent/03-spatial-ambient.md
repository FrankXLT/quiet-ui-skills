# Brand Blueprint — 03 Spatial & Ambient Living (Disabled Scope)

## 1. Architectural Scope Decision
The Brand Starter explicitly disables personal and ambient residential tiers:
- **13. App-5 (Wellness & Restorative Companion):** Disabled (`disabled_tiers`).
- **15. HA-1 (Ambient Wall Screensaver):** Disabled (`disabled_tiers`).
- **16. HA-2 (Room-First Wall Control Panel):** Disabled (`disabled_tiers`).
- **17. HA-3 (Foldable & Phone Companion):** Disabled (`disabled_tiers`).

## 2. Rationale
Corporate platforms, B2B SaaS applications, and developer document portals do not require residential smart-home device control or ambient artwork rendering. Omission of these tiers reduces showcase bundle size by over 80% and eliminates extraneous multimedia payloads while keeping navigation strictly focused on business and technical workflows.
