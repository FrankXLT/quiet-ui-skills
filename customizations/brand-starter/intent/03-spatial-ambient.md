# Brand Blueprint — 03 Specialized & Ambient Workspaces (Disabled Scope)

## 1. Architectural Scope Decision
The Brand Starter explicitly disables personal wellness, optical HUD, and tactile console tiers:
- **13. App-5 (Workload & Restorative Companion):** Disabled (`disabled_tiers`).
- **15. HUD-1 (Optical Viewport / AR HUD):** Disabled (`disabled_tiers`).
- **16. Ctrl-1 (Tactile Subsystem Console):** Disabled (`disabled_tiers`).

## 2. Rationale
Corporate platforms, B2B SaaS applications, and developer document portals do not require mission-control optical HUD viewports or specialized physical effector consoles. Omission of these tiers reduces showcase bundle size by over 60% and eliminates extraneous multimedia payloads while keeping navigation strictly focused on enterprise business and technical workflows.
