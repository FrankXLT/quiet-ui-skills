# Brand Blueprint — 02 Workspaces & Applications

## 1. Scope & Tiers
Powers core enterprise product capabilities, data exploration, and team management across 7 tiers:
- **07. Deck-1 (Executive Briefing):** Slide-deck layouts for board presentations and project kickoffs.
- **08. Bridge-2 (Fleet & Data Grid):** 1px-bordered high-density tabular ledgers with 3-zone cards.
- **09. App-2 (Live Observability Console):** Strip-chart metrics and streaming time-series graphs.
- **10. App-1 (Issue & Backlog Tracker):** 36px triage rows with detailed slide-out inspector panels.
- **11. App-4 (Sprint & Temporal Planner):** Roadmap execution with time blocks, daily status briefings, and priority badges.
- **12. App-3 (Executive KPI Bento):** 2x2 executive bento grid showcasing organizational health metrics.
- **14. App-Tree (Schema & File Explorer):** Coupled 3-pane schema/data tree with SQL workbenches.

## 2. Invariants
Every number in tabular views enforces `tabular-nums` formatting to prevent layout jitter during data updates.
